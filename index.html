About Me

I'm Mochammad Ijdin Rozaqi, an Ocean Engineering graduate from Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (GPA 3.68/4.00). I bring dedication, curiosity, and a genuine passion for learning to every project — currently applying that as a Marine Engineer at PT. ZEE Engineering Indonesia, after starting out as an Intern Surveyor at Biro Klasifikasi Indonesia.

My thesis analyzed the design and strength of vertical turbine reservoir structures for energy utilization in open canals — a project that took me from concept sketches through Ansys-based structural analysis. Safety runs through everything I do: I hold the AK3 Umum certification plus thirteen more HSE and technical credentials.

THESIS & RESEARCH

Design Case Study

How one structure went from a canal survey to a validated design.

Design and Strength Analysis of Vertical Turbine Reservoir Structures for energy utilization in open canals — B.Eng. Thesis, Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember.

01
📐Site Surveyimages/thesis-01.jpg

Conceptual Design

Started from a location survey of the open canal, translating site limitations and regulatory constraints into a first design concept — calculations worked in parallel with AutoCAD drafting.

02
📐2D Drawingimages/thesis-02.jpg

2D Concept Design

Locked the concept into two-dimensional drawings that reflected every constraint identified during the survey.

03
🧱3D Modelimages/thesis-03.jpg

3D Design Model

Converted the 2D concept into a 3D model to see how the structure would actually stand in place.

04
🖼️Visualizationimages/thesis-04.jpg

Structure Illustration

Built a fuller visualization of the structure standing within the canal, for a clearer read on scale and placement.

05
🔧Refined Modelimages/thesis-05.jpg

Refined Model

Layered in further calculations and engineering approaches to refine the design beyond the initial concept.

06
⚗️Experimentimages/thesis-06.jpg

Experiment

Ran physical experiments to capture the real load acting on the structure, grounding the analysis in measured data.

07
📊Strength Checkimages/thesis-07.jpg

Structural Analysis

Tested whether the structure was strong enough to function reliably as a power-generating structure.

08
🌀Deformationimages/thesis-08.jpg

Deformation (Ansys)

Simulated deformation under load in Ansys to verify how the structure would behave under real operating conditions.

09
🌀Von-Misesimages/thesis-09.jpg

Von-Mises Analysis (Ansys)

Ran Von-Mises stress analysis on the turbine in Ansys to confirm the final design held up under stress.

WHY I DO THIS

"A structure doesn't care how confident your calculation was — only whether it holds."

That's the standard I hold my own work to. I'm a recent Ocean Engineering graduate driven by curiosity and a genuine appetite for learning — eager to bring fresh eyes and real solutions to any team I join, and excited by the challenge of thriving in a fast-moving professional environment.

My path so far has taken me from inspecting ships against classification rules at Biro Klasifikasi Indonesia to running mooring and structural analysis on offshore projects at PT. ZEE Engineering Indonesia — two vantage points on the same question: will this hold up in the real world? I plan to keep chasing that question.
